# Maven Toys Store — Power BI Sales & Inventory Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is a Power BI analysis of **Maven Toys**, a fictional chain of toy stores operating across Mexico.

The project was completed from the perspective of a **Business Intelligence Consultant** brought in to analyze sales and inventory data and identify patterns that could support future store expansion.

The analysis combines sales, product, store, and inventory data to provide an overview of business performance and highlight trends across time, product categories, locations, and individual stores.

## 🎯 Business Objective

Maven Toys is considering expanding its store network and needs data-driven insights to support its decisions.

The objective of this project was to analyze the available business data and build an interactive Power BI dashboard that answers questions such as:

* How much revenue and profit is the business generating?
* How many units and orders are being made?
* How do sales change over time?
* Which product categories generate the most sales?
* Which stores contribute the most to sales?
* Where is inventory concentrated?
* How does stock on hand vary across locations and cities?
* What patterns in sales and inventory could be relevant when considering future expansion?

## 📁 Dataset

The dataset contains fictional business data for Maven Toys, including:

* Daily sales transactions
* Product information
* Store information
* Inventory levels
* Product categories
* Store locations and cities

The original dataset was provided as part of the **Maven Toys Challenge** by Maven Analytics.

## 📈 Dashboard

The Power BI report contains **4 pages of interactive visualizations** covering business performance, sales trends, products, stores, and inventory.

### Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard includes KPI cards for:

* **Total Profit**
* **Total Revenue**
* **Total Orders**
* **Units Sold**
* **Number of Stores**
* **Number of Products**

### Sales Analysis

The report analyzes sales performance through:

* **Sales by Month** — showing changes and trends in sales over time.
* **Sales by Product Category** — comparing performance across different toy categories.
* **Sales by Store Name** — highlighting differences in sales performance between individual stores.

### Inventory Analysis

Inventory performance is explored through:

* **Stock on Hand by Location**
* **Stock on Hand by City**

These visuals provide a view of how available inventory is distributed across the business.
